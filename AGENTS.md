# Public repository

Treat everything committed here as public, including tests, fixtures, snapshots, examples, documentation, and commit messages. Use invented data rather than copying a real session or merely changing its most obvious identifiers.

Use temporary directories for filesystem tests, fictional paths such as `/workspace/demo` for display fixtures, and `example.com` for sample addresses. Keep personal details, machine-specific paths, private hostnames, credentials, and raw logs or transcripts out of the repository. Before committing, check the staged diff and new files (including generated output and images) for accidental disclosures.
